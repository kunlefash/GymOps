Evaluation Guide

Review each direction against these criteria. Directions are not mutually exclusive — FitBase will use different directions for different screens:

D1 + D3
Staff shift home screen — choose between warm (D1) or dark (D3)
D2 + D6
Member status home — choose between full-bleed hero (D2) or warm card (D6)
D4
Payment recording drawer — core interaction, likely no variation needed
D5
Payment success — the defining moment. Full-screen green is non-negotiable.
D7
Owner dashboard — dark stats header + expiry alerts + bottom nav
D8
Onboarding wizard — one-question-per-screen, option cards, progress bar
